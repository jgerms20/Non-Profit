# SCAiL Pilot Session — Intake Survey Options

Three survey approaches to gauge AI knowledge, attitudes, and usage across cohorts.

**Cohorts:**
- Cohort A: Younger Adults (20–35)
- Cohort B: Working Adults (35–60)
- Cohort C: Older Adults (60+)

---

## OPTION 1: Quick & Casual (5–7 minutes)

Best for: Getting people in the door without intimidating them. Low friction. Works well sent via text.

### Questions

1. **What's your name?** (Short answer)

2. **Which age group are you in?**
   - 20–35
   - 35–60
   - 60+

3. **What do you do for work (or what did you do)?** (Short answer)

4. **How would you describe your comfort level with technology in general?**
   - Very comfortable — I'm usually the one helping others
   - Comfortable — I use it daily without much trouble
   - Okay — I get by but I'm not super confident
   - Not very comfortable — I avoid it when I can

5. **Have you ever used an AI tool? (ChatGPT, Siri, Google Assistant, Alexa, etc.)**
   - Yes, regularly
   - Yes, a few times
   - I've heard of them but haven't really used one
   - I'm not sure what counts as AI

6. **In one sentence, what comes to mind when you hear "artificial intelligence"?** (Short answer)

7. **What would you most want to learn about AI?** (Check all that apply)
   - How to use it for my job or business
   - How to use it for everyday tasks (writing, planning, searching)
   - How it actually works (the basics)
   - How to know if something is AI-generated
   - How to protect myself from AI scams/misinfo
   - I'm just curious — open to anything

8. **Any concerns or fears about AI you'd want us to address?** (Short answer, optional)

9. **Best way to reach you for scheduling?** (Email / Phone / Text)

## OPTION 2: Detailed Intake (10–12 minutes)

Best for: Getting rich data you can use for curriculum design and grant reporting. Good for people who've committed to participating.

### Section 1: About You

1. **Full name** (Short answer)

2. **Age range**
   - 20–25
   - 26–30
   - 31–35
   - 36–45
   - 46–55
   - 56–65
   - 66–75
   - 75+

3. **City/Town in South Carolina** (Short answer)

4. **Current employment status**
   - Employed full-time
   - Employed part-time
   - Self-employed / Business owner
   - Looking for work
   - Student
   - Retired
   - Other

5. **What's your job title or field?** (Short answer)

6. **Highest level of education completed**
   - Some high school
   - High school diploma / GED
   - Some college or trade school
   - Associate's degree
   - Bachelor's degree
   - Master's degree or higher

### Section 2: Technology Comfort

7. **How often do you use the internet?**
   - Multiple times a day
   - Once a day
   - A few times a week
   - Rarely
   - Never

8. **Which devices do you use regularly?** (Check all)
   - Smartphone
   - Laptop
   - Desktop computer
   - Tablet
   - Smart speaker (Alexa, Google Home)
   - None of the above

9. **Rate your overall technology confidence (1–5)**
   - 1 = I struggle with basic tasks
   - 3 = I can do what I need to but nothing fancy
   - 5 = I'm very tech-savvy

### Section 3: AI Knowledge & Attitudes

10. **Before today, how would you rate your understanding of AI?**
    - I don't really know what AI is
    - I've heard of it but couldn't explain it
    - I have a basic understanding
    - I understand it well
    - I work with AI or have studied it

11. **Which of these have you used?** (Check all that apply)
    - ChatGPT / Claude / Gemini
    - Siri or Google Assistant
    - Alexa or smart home devices
    - AI writing tools (Grammarly, etc.)
    - AI image generators (DALL-E, Midjourney)
    - AI in social media (TikTok recommendations, etc.)
    - None that I know of

12. **If you've used AI, what did you use it for?** (Short answer, optional)

13. **How do you feel about AI overall?** (Select one)
    - Excited — I think it's going to help people
    - Curious — I want to know more before I decide
    - Neutral — I don't have strong feelings
    - Cautious — I think there are real risks
    - Worried — I think it's mostly dangerous

14. **What concerns you most about AI?** (Check all that apply)
    - It might take jobs away
    - I don't understand how it works
    - It could be used to manipulate or scam people
    - It feels like it's moving too fast
    - Privacy and data collection
    - Bias and unfairness in AI systems
    - Nothing — I'm not really concerned
    - Other: ___

15. **What excites you most about AI?** (Check all that apply)
    - Saving time on repetitive tasks
    - Learning new things faster
    - Starting or growing a business
    - Helping my community
    - Getting a better job or advancing my career
    - Creating content (art, writing, music)
    - Nothing excites me about it yet
    - Other: ___

### Section 4: What You Want

16. **What would make this session worthwhile for you?** (Short answer)

17. **Is there anything specific you'd want to be able to DO with AI after this session?** (Short answer)

18. **Any accessibility needs we should know about?** (Large text, hearing assistance, etc.) (Short answer, optional)

19. **Preferred contact method** (Email / Phone / Text)

20. **Email or phone number** (Short answer)

## OPTION 3: Conversational / Storytelling Format (8–10 minutes)

Best for: Older adults or people who might be put off by a traditional survey. Feels more like a conversation. Great for qualitative data and quotes you can use in grant applications.

### Questions

1. **What's your name and where in South Carolina are you from?** (Short answer)

2. **Which group fits you best?**
   - 20–35 years old
   - 35–60 years old
   - 60 and up

3. **Tell us a little about yourself — what do you do (or what did you do for work)?** (Paragraph)

4. **When you hear "AI" or "artificial intelligence," what's the first thing that pops into your head?** (Paragraph)

5. **Have you ever used something with AI and thought "whoa, that's actually useful" — or "that's actually scary"? Tell us about it.** (Paragraph)

6. **On a scale of 1–10, how confident do you feel using technology day-to-day?**
   (1 = not at all, 10 = totally confident)

7. **If someone told you "AI can help you with [blank]," what would you fill in the blank with? What do you WISH AI could help you with?** (Paragraph)

8. **What's one thing about AI that makes you nervous, skeptical, or uncomfortable?** (Paragraph)

9. **Have you ever felt "left behind" when it comes to technology? What happened?** (Paragraph)

10. **If you could sit down with an AI expert for 30 minutes and ask them anything, what would you ask?** (Paragraph)

11. **What would make you feel like this session was time well spent?** (Short answer)

12. **Anything else you want us to know before we meet?** (Paragraph, optional)

13. **Best way to reach you?** (Email / Phone / Text + contact info)

---

## Recommendations by Cohort

| Cohort | Recommended Option | Why |
|--------|-------------------|-----|
| **Younger Adults (20–35)** | Option 1 or Option 2 | They're used to forms, won't mind checkboxes, and you'll get structured data fast. Option 1 if recruiting via text, Option 2 if they've already committed. |
| **Working Adults (35–60)** | Option 2 | This group will give you the richest career/job context. The detailed version captures their professional needs. |
| **Older Adults (60+)** | Option 3 | Open-ended questions feel less like a test. Lets them share stories. You'll get powerful quotes for grants and marketing. |

---

## Bonus: Universal Add-On Questions

These work for any cohort and can be tacked onto any option above:

- **How did you hear about SCAiL?** (Word of mouth / Social media / Church or community org / Friend or family / Other)
- **Would you be interested in future sessions or programs?** (Yes / Maybe / No)
- **Would you be willing to share your experience with others after the session?** (Testimonial, social media post, etc.)
- **Do you know anyone else who might benefit from a session like this?** (Name/contact, optional)

---

## Notes for Building in Google Forms

- Use **Section breaks** to keep it from feeling overwhelming
- Add a **header image** with SCAiL branding (navy + gold)
- Set paragraph questions to **optional** so nobody gets stuck
- Use **page logic** if mixing cohorts in one form (branch by age group)
- Add a **confirmation message**: "Thank you! We're excited to learn alongside you. You'll hear from us within 48 hours with session details."
