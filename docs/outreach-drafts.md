## 4. Board Recruitment One-Pager

**Use when:** Reaching out to potential board members or sharing with warm connections who might refer candidates.

### The SCAiL Initiative — Join Our Founding Board

**Mission.** We teach AI literacy to communities across South Carolina — starting with the people who usually get overlooked. From there, we grow into an incubator and a fund that turns learners into founders, and founders into funded companies.

**The three-phase flywheel.**

1. **AI Literacy Nonprofit (501(c)(3))** — Teach communities. Build trust. Generate grant revenue.
2. **Community Innovation Incubator** — Run hackathons, cohorts, and demo days for founders from those communities.
3. **Investment Fund / Angel Network** — Fund the strongest startups from the incubator. Generate returns. Reinvest in Phase 1.

**Where we are right now.** Articles of Incorporation filed with the SC Secretary of State. Registered agent secured. First workshops planned for May. Full curriculum (8 courses) built. Brand system built. Public website and tracker live.

**Why now.** South Carolina is hungry for community-level AI education and nobody is doing it at scale. Universities are doing research. Libraries are doing one-offs. No one is building a statewide, free, community-embedded AI literacy program. SCAiL is that program.

### Three Open Board Roles

We're recruiting three founding board members with complementary skill sets.

**1. Legal / Governance Chair**
- Experience in nonprofit formation, 501(c)(3) compliance, or corporate governance
- Helps us navigate Form 1023, bylaws, conflict of interest policies, and ongoing compliance
- Time commitment: 4–6 hours/month, more in founding months

**2. Finance / Treasurer**
- Experience in nonprofit finance, accounting, or financial oversight
- Oversees budget, grant reporting, banking, and financial controls
- Time commitment: 4–6 hours/month

**3. Education / Curriculum Chair**
- Experience in K–12, higher ed, adult learning, or community education
- Helps us refine curriculum, measure outcomes, and build the Train-the-Trainer program
- Time commitment: 4–6 hours/month

### What Board Members Get

- A meaningful role in a mission-driven organization from day one
- Real impact on South Carolina communities
- A professional network built around civic technology and AI equity
- A résumé and portfolio piece few others will have
- A founding-board title that matters if Phase 2 and Phase 3 succeed

### What We Need From Board Members

- Active participation in quarterly board meetings
- Specific contribution in their area of expertise
- Willingness to open doors to funders, partners, and advisors
- A genuine belief that every community deserves AI literacy

### Next Steps

If you're interested — or know someone who would be — reach out directly to Joshua German at [email] or [phone]. A first conversation is 30 minutes, no commitment.

*Thank you for considering joining us. This work matters, and it matters most if we build it together.*
